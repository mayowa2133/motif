# Motif gold quality contract v1

Opt in with `quality_mode: motif-gold-v1`. VoiceStudio v6 is the accepted visual benchmark; its claims are not factual evidence. Preserve the default style. See [QUALITY_SYSTEM.md](QUALITY_SYSTEM.md) for executable gates.

| Critical gate | Required painted evidence |
|---|---|
| story | Each major beat has a subject, action, visible before and after, focal detail, and consequential change. |
| cause-effect | A visible cause precedes its result; labels reinforce evidence. |
| focal-hierarchy | The feature explaining the current sentence commands attention. |
| character-performance | Participating Bot's posture and silhouette express its role at native mobile size. |
| physicality | Important contact produces an appropriate local response; grips track anchors. |
| energy | Active shots carry purposeful anticipation, reactions, residual movement or overlap. A completed action followed by a dead caption hold fails. |
| art | Hero has a distinctive silhouette, material identity and physical layering. |
| composition | Intentional staging and depth; avoid repeatedly using a generic rectangle, tiny Bot and empty field. |
| mobile | Essential meaning survives at 360 × 640 without captions. |
| continuity | Persistent objects remain identifiable; the ending pays off the opening. |

No numeric average. Each gate is PASS, FAIL or NOT_ASSESSED. Any FAIL or missing assessment blocks advancement. Character performance can be NOT_APPLICABLE only when Bot is explicitly absent. Silence, missing reports and a successful encode are not approval.

Rough moving previews, with and without captions, must clear both independent critics before final artwork. Final moving QA must clear them again before audio finishing. Technical gates are separate and must also pass. A human must watch and approve the final video; the system never publishes or grants approval.

Readability, canonical geometry, anchors, materials and rights remain governed by `docs/STYLE_BIBLE.md` and `docs/ASSET_SPEC.md`. New artwork or choreography is allowed when explicitly identified as agent-assisted development. Review it before depending on it across shots.
