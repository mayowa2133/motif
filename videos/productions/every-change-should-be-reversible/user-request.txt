I checked the pushed v1.1 implementation on GitHub, including the updated schema, bindings, repair logic, temporal-evidence changes, and validation notes. **I’d approve Quality System v1.1 and stop working on infrastructure for now.**

The three issues I was worried about are genuinely addressed:

- **Art-only repairs now count** without forcing fake plan/event changes. `repair()` compares semantic state and render-state fingerprints separately. [motif_quality.py](https://github.com/mayowa2133/motif/blob/65ce7d907e4d7d1048d8523af05211b7b7c71562/scripts/motif_quality.py)
- **The energy schema no longer forces fake motion.** Only `dominant_action` is mandatory; the other channels can be null, and minimal-isolated scenes are explicitly representable. [shot-contract.schema.json](https://github.com/mayowa2133/motif/blob/65ce7d907e4d7d1048d8523af05211b7b7c71562/schemas/shot-contract.schema.json)
- **Renderer support broadened honestly.** Interactive UI, calm paper, and limited workshop bindings are now explicit, while unsafe contacts still fail instead of being silently ignored. [bindings.json](https://github.com/mayowa2133/motif/blob/65ce7d907e4d7d1048d8523af05211b7b7c71562/quality/bindings.json)

The dense temporal strips are also a meaningful improvement. Catching a three-frame detachment that normal before/contact/after samples would miss is exactly why that change mattered. The committed evidence documents that the new critics caught it. [v1.1 validation](https://github.com/mayowa2133/motif/blob/65ce7d907e4d7d1048d8523af05211b7b7c71562/quality/validation/v1.1/README.md)

The workshop limitation is fine. **Explicitly limited support is much better than pretending Motif can safely animate every attachment.**

Now comes the real test.
