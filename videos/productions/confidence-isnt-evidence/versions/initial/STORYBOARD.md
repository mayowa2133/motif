# Confidence Isn't Evidence — proposed storyboard

Status: REVIEW_REQUIRED

Rationale: A compact paper investigation makes support relationships tangible: the bot opens, traces, compares, and cautiously files each layer. One conspicuous empty connector remains unresolved, so the payoff is a readable understanding of the claim’s limits rather than a verdict.

Narration is the supplied script, unchanged. Planning: live Codex; geometry/choreography: agent-assisted finite shared bindings.
