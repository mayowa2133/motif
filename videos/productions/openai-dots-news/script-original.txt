OpenAI just gave ChatGPT a tiny coworker with its own computer — and it doesn’t clock out. They’re called Dots. Instead of living inside one chat bubble, a Dot can connect to your apps, use its own cloud computer, and keep chewing through a multi-step job in the background. OpenAI is pitching them for real work and personal errands, with specialist Dots for companies, and teams of Dots planned later. But here’s the catch: early hands-on testing found public-site security checks can still stop them, and right now Dots are tied to the $100-a-month Pro tier while Meta’s Muse is free. So the next AI race isn’t just who has the smartest chatbot. It’s who gets to become your always-on digital coworker. Would you give one the keys?
