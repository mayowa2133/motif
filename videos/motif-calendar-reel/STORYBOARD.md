# Demo 03 — production storyboard

This is the revised plan saved **before** finished animation or render. The untouched [initial storyboard](STORYBOARD_INITIAL.md) and [pre-render review](PLANNING_REVIEW.md) show the single planning correction. The persistent prop is one tactile paper calendar; the two solid event cards are the real schedule, while the pale dashed slip is only a proposal. The script is illustrative and names no product.

Narration: “Two plans land at the same time. An assistant spots the overlap and suggests a free slot. But the calendar stays put. It waits for your approval. Only then does the appointment move.” Approximate five-beat duration: 3 + 2.5 + 3.5 + 3 + 4 = 16 seconds. Final timings follow the natural Kokoro take.

## Frame 1 — Two plans collide, approximately 0–3 s

- status: outline
- scene: Teal CALL and coral FOCUS appointment cards slide into one 3 PM row on a tactile paper planner; their edges collide.
- voiceover: “Two plans land at the same time.”

**Subject:** two solid appointment cards. **Action:** both claim the same 3 PM space. **Visible before/after:** one card → two overlapped cards. **Focal detail:** their shared row and compressed card edges, readable on a phone. **Consequence:** Bot notices the conflict. The second arrival makes a paper bump; no “CONFLICT” label is required.

## Frame 2 — Bot notices, approximately 3–5.5 s

- status: outline
- scene: Bot points at the overlap; a physical coral bracket hugs the occupied 3 PM row.
- voiceover: “An assistant spots the overlap…”

**Subject:** the occupied 3 PM row. **Action:** Bot identifies the interference. **Visible before/after:** unmarked pile → bracket framing the trapped edges. **Focal detail:** the collision, shown closer than the whole calendar. **Consequence:** Bot looks for another slot. Keep one edge of the planner and Bot's pointing hand in the close view so the detail remains part of the physical world.

## Frame 3 — A proposal, not a change, approximately 5.5–9 s

- status: outline
- scene: The solid coral card stays at 3 PM. Bot places a separate pale, dashed FOCUS proposal slip into the empty 4 PM row, with a dotted route from the real card and a large “4 PM?” note.
- voiceover: “…and suggests a free slot. But the calendar stays put.”

**Subject:** a proposed position for the coral event. **Action:** Bot offers a different time. **Visible before/after:** 4 PM empty → pale dashed possible placement at 4 PM, while the real overlap remains. **Focal detail:** simultaneous visibility of the solid card still at 3 PM and its dashed proposal at 4 PM. **Consequence:** human decision is required. The dashed slip must look distinct from a booked event.

## Frame 4 — A person decides, approximately 9–12 s

- status: outline
- scene: Bot holds back. A separate human fingertip enters and presses a tactile APPROVE tab beside the proposal. The original cards remain overlapped until the press completes.
- voiceover: “It waits for your approval.”

**Subject:** the person's approval action. **Action:** fingertip physically presses the tab. **Visible before/after:** unpressed tab, solid conflict unchanged → pressed tab. **Focal detail:** finger-to-tab contact and still-overlapped 3 PM cards in the same view. **Consequence:** permission now exists to modify the real schedule. No self-pressing button or premature event motion.

## Frame 5 — The schedule changes, approximately 12–16 s

- status: outline
- scene: After approval, the real coral FOCUS card slides from 3 PM to 4 PM, replacing the dashed proposal slip. The bracket releases and both solid cards settle into separate rows.
- voiceover: “Only then does the appointment move.”

**Subject:** the real schedule. **Action:** the solid card moves. **Visible before/after:** overlap with a proposed open slot → two booked events in separate rows. **Focal detail:** the route of the moving solid card and the two clear final rows. **Consequence:** the approved change is complete. End on the readable planner, with a small Bot reaction only after the calendar settles.

## Production notes

Use one continuous paper-calendar world, moving from a brief wide view into meaningful close views and back to the final schedule. The connection between card, Bot, and person stays visible through physical interaction. The approved Motif Bot, palette, matte cut edges, down-right shadows, caption baseline, event system, Kokoro `af_nova` narration, and cleared SFX remain. New art is limited to what the calendar story needs. No new motion engine action or broad prop library is planned. At 360 × 640, the overlap, ghost proposal, finger press, and final movement must read before any caption is considered.
