# Demo 03 — pre-render self-review

Reviewed the saved [initial storyboard](STORYBOARD_INITIAL.md) against [the Motif storyboard gate](../../docs/STYLE_BIBLE.md#storyboard-gate-before-rendering) before building the animation.

## Finding and bounded revision

The initial Frame 3 lifted the **real** FOCUS card toward 4 PM before the person approved. Even if the narration called it a suggestion, that physical move could imply the schedule had already changed. This misrepresents the core permission distinction.

In the revised [production storyboard](STORYBOARD.md), the real coral card stays visibly trapped at 3 PM. Bot moves a **pale, dashed paper proposal slip** toward the empty 4 PM row. A dotted path connects the real card to that possible position. The proposal remains separate from the calendar's solid event cards. The fingertip presses the approval tab while both the original overlap and the proposed slot are visible. Only after the press does the solid coral card slide to 4 PM; the proposal slip disappears as the move completes.

The production narration also makes the unchanged schedule explicit (“But the calendar stays put”) and uses a longer natural sentence to give the visible proposal and approval room within the requested 12–18 second range. The original shorter script remains in the untouched initial storyboard.

## Gate result

- **Subject, action, before/after, focal detail, consequence:** specified in each beat of the production storyboard.
- **Text genuinely needed:** 3 PM and 4 PM labels orient the viewer; CALL and FOCUS distinguish commitments; APPROVE names the human decision. Captions reinforce the story but do not carry the overlap, proposal, or authorization alone.
- **Visible outcome:** two solid cards overlap at 3 PM; a pale dashed copy marks a possible 4 PM position; a human finger presses approval; then one solid card moves and the overlap clears.
- **Reuse:** canonical Bot, approved warm wall and floor, tactile paper treatment, stable caption component, event engine, Kokoro narration workflow, and cleared paper/click effects.
- **Minimum new art:** calendar board with two slots, event-card template, proposal overlay, physical approval tab, and a simplified human fingertip. These are story-specific pieces, not a speculative library.

No second concept was developed. Proceed with the revised single story and inspect the first encoded render for mobile-scale causality and timing.
