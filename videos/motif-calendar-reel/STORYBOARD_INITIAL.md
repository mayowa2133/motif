# Demo 03 — initial pre-render storyboard

This is the first saved plan, before production art or animation. The persistent prop is a large tactile paper planner with a 3 PM row and an empty 4 PM row. Two distinct appointment cards share the 3 PM row. Small labels identify times and commitments; the overlap and later move must read through card placement. The canonical Bot is the assistant; a separate human fingertip operates the approval control. The script is illustrative and names no product.

Proposed narration: “Two plans. One time. An assistant spots the overlap and suggests a free slot. It waits for your approval. Only then does the calendar change.”

## Frame 1 — Collision, approximately 0–3 s

- status: outline
- scene: A warm paper calendar fills the frame; a teal CALL card and a coral FOCUS card slide into the same 3 PM space and collide.
- voiceover: “Two plans. One time.”

Subject: the two appointment cards. Action: both claim one slot. Before/after: one card at 3 PM → two visibly stacked there. Focal detail: the shared 3 PM row and bent, overlapping card edges. Consequence: Bot notices the collision. Sound: two paper taps, second with a soft bump. Avoid a floating “CONFLICT” label as the explanation.

## Frame 2 — Notice, approximately 3–5.5 s

- status: outline
- scene: Bot points at the trapped overlapping edges while a coral bracket hugs the 3 PM row.
- voiceover: “An assistant spots the overlap…”

Subject: the occupied slot. Action: Bot identifies the interference. Before/after: an unmarked pile → a physical bracket around the overlap. Focal detail: the compressed edges, framed closer than the whole planner. Consequence: search for an open row. Sound: small paper tick. Do not cut to an unrelated interface.

## Frame 3 — Suggested opening, approximately 5.5–9 s

- status: outline
- scene: Bot lifts the coral FOCUS card toward the empty 4 PM row, leaving a dotted arrow and a “4 PM?” suggestion tag.
- voiceover: “…and suggests a free slot.”

Subject: the coral card. Action: Bot shows a possible later position. Before/after: 4 PM empty → proposed position at 4 PM. Focal detail: the empty 4 PM row aligned with the proposed card. Consequence: the person must decide. Sound: light paper slide. Do not show a success check yet.

## Frame 4 — Wait for permission, approximately 9–12 s

- status: outline
- scene: Bot holds a paper APPROVE tab; a human fingertip enters and presses it.
- voiceover: “It waits for your approval.”

Subject: the approval tab. Action: Bot offers, person presses. Before/after: untouched tab → pressed tab. Focal detail: the fingertip contacting the tab, with the planner still visible. Consequence: only now may the appointment move. Sound: tactile click. Do not let the button act by itself.

## Frame 5 — Calendar clears, approximately 12–16 s

- status: outline
- scene: The coral card settles into 4 PM, the bracket releases 3 PM, and the two appointments end in separate rows. Bot relaxes.
- voiceover: “Only then does the calendar change.”

Subject: the real schedule. Action: the card moves to the open slot. Before/after: overlap → two separated commitments. Focal detail: the now clear 3 PM and occupied 4 PM rows shown together. Consequence: a readable, approved plan. Sound: paper slide and small settle. Avoid a celebration that obscures the finished schedule.

## Planning constraints

Use the approved Motif world and canonical Bot, without changing the two prior demos. Reuse wall/floor and paper caption treatment. New art should be limited to planner, two appointment card variants, approval tab, and human fingertip. Keep all large story geometry legible at 360 × 640. Keep tiny calendar text secondary to card position and hand action. No music is planned; short SFX leave space for narration.
