# Directing by visible state changes

This guide distills decisions from completed, separately reviewed film studies. It is a set of directing questions, not an approved asset catalogue or a claim that Motif can choose equally effective scenes for an unseen script. The studies used substantial authored geometry, choreography and timing. Mechanical regression tests establish implementation behavior; they do not establish creative transfer, sound quality or overall film quality.

## Choose the inference before the prop

For each important script proposition, write what a viewer should understand with captions hidden. Identify the starting condition, the action that changes it, and the visible result. Different labels on an unchanged card often leave the relationship unexplained.

Consider two plausible ways to expose the relationship: interaction, transformation, burden removal, test, inspection, comparison, routing, interface action or a callback. Choose according to the claim's verb and the viewer's context. These are options, not a quota of mechanisms or sets. A sustained interface with a changed input, pending state and changed response can explain a process more clearly than several unrelated machines.

Give the actor an explicit role: participant, reactor, presenter or absent. Identify who causes the change. Preserve useful identity across a handoff or transformation, including a selected token, input/output object or tested result. Explain both ends of a connection and show activation after contact.

## Stage the decisive states

Inspect caption-free before, contact and after states at native size and at the delivery size. The receiving surface, support, ownership and occlusion must explain the action. A strike needs contact before release; an input needs a visible receiver before a response. A continuous transformation should retain object identity while making the resulting state readable.

Keep one clear hero region while its semantic object or state develops. Show specific examples before compressing them into a shared system. Return to an opening object in a changed relationship when that resolves the film's claim. Illustrative interfaces, numbers and connections remain illustrations; their depiction does not verify a product or integration claim.

## Time the result against speech

Use measured speech cues where available and preserve uncertainty in automatic alignment. Caption onset is not a measured spoken-word onset. The visible result must be present by the phrase that depends on it; advancing an animation by an arbitrary offset may still leave the result late.

Review consecutive frames around contact, landing and handoff. Sparse before/after samples can miss a brief separation or overlapping states. Exact encoded-frame inspection establishes the inspected states and timing, while uninterrupted playback and direct listening require separate evidence.

## Freeze, diagnose and bound the repair

Render a complete moving first film early and preserve it with its inputs. Diagnose whether the defect lies in contact, readability, timing or the scene idea. Repair a concrete mechanical cause; reconsider the relationship when repeated repairs still leave the claim unexplained.

Keep the initial assessment and subsequent independent findings separately. A later review can supersede a stopping rationale without rewriting its history. Record the changed windows, unchanged material and remaining gaps. Stop when there is no justified current intervention likely to improve the identified problem; neither a fixed revision count nor a technical PASS proves an optimal concept.

Unrendered alternatives remain hypotheses. A reconstruction informed by released source pictures does not validate autonomous direction on a new script. New mascot, style, scene and asset candidates require their own review before promotion. This consolidation promotes no creative assets.

## Provenance and limits

This is original reusable prose synthesized from the final directing guide and frozen blind-result report of the completed studies. Source-specific footage, audio, frames, artwork, private paths and study implementation are excluded. Reported repairs are observations within those studies, not newly reproduced creative experiments. The transferable questions above still need evaluation on an unseen script.

encoded-review protocol from later completed repairs. Its novel-film claims remain
hypotheses. The [optional experiment gate](../quality/experiment-gate/README.md)
keeps scoped evidence, disagreements and rollback separate from production defaults.
