# Concept simplification / hero succession v1

This adds planning and static evidence requirements above Structural Grammar v1 and Quality System v1.1. It retains the registered private corpus and existing renderer. It does not promote a concept to a finished-film approval.

## Semantic review

The structure critic must inspect actual beat heroes, actions, before/after, and required mechanisms. `COMPOUND_VISUAL_RULE` blocks a semantically related passage when its substantially different physical rules require new apparatus and hero relationships. Many states of one process remain valid; no verb/slash/prop count is used. `UNNECESSARY_VISIBLE_MECHANISM` blocks inactive apparatus competing with the current hero. Geometry may crop, fold, dim or leave frame while one token carries continuity. `ACTOR_AMBIGUITY` blocks unclear agency, not the mere presence of two actors. Identify subject, causal actor and Bot role; products may act while Bot is absent or reacts.

New ordinary planner context and independent structure/concept reviews include these principles. Frozen records are retained unchanged; changed policy hashes require new live review for subsequent work.

## Executable concept contract

`scripts/motif_concept.py prepare --project <production>` generates a live, data-only `concept-contract.json` from actual beats, validates its schema/beat roles, retrieves at most two actual setup packages per critical beat, and invokes an independent beat Reference Director with their decoded frames. Each beat records subject, actor, Bot role, physical rule, current hero, geometry to prune and every planned interaction. Retrieval is keyed to semantic relationships, not product nouns. Selected evidence is private, never artwork or a factual source. Missing relationship evidence blocks.

The concept gate automatically prepares the contract if required and absent. Artwork must supply caption-free 360×640 BEFORE, CONTACT and AFTER images for every listed interaction. `contact_proofs` lists the exact beat/interaction IDs and frame file/hash for each state. Temporary markers may exist in separate diagnostics, but cannot substitute for clean proofs. Integrity checks enforce coverage, dimensions, clean flags and hashes. The live independent critic judges whether actor, surface, support, ownership and blocked/unblocked state actually read. `CONTACT_PROOF_UNREADABLE` or NOT_ASSESSED blocks.

## Bounded scope and deadlock

`scripts/motif_concept.py replan --project <new-concept-cycle> --original <preserved-production> --setup <blocked-setup>` makes a live patch. It can simplify, change archetype or split, while enforcing exact narration, beat order, outgoing boundary and unchanged other setups/beats. Only affected continuity-token lifecycle entries may change. The merge also retires superseded shared rationale, unused resource IDs and phase/actor requirements; new active requirements come from the accepted patch. Assets used exclusively by locked beats remain unchanged. No model code executes. New art remains explicitly agent-assisted development.

Two distinct completed live concept reviews failing the same hero/contact/hierarchy check trigger `CONCEPT_DEADLOCK`. Copied/aborted reviews do not count. A third cosmetic review of that family is blocked. Start a scoped cycle with a validated simplification/archetype change/split; moving repair budget is separate. Prior concepts/reports stay in place and repeated reviews are archived before replacement.

A scoped cycle may inherit passing previews byte-for-byte. The gate checks their original plan, beat contracts, preview hashes and prior passing assessment. It reviews only the replacement setups; inherited setups do not receive new motion approval. An unscoped cycle requires review/proofs for every setup. Evidence includes source hashes and bound contract/scope/retrieval records so later changes invalidate PASS.
