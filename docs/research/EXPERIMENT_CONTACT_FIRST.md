Status: research hypothesis; live planning underway. Not a claimed reusable fix or accepted film.

## Why this test

The current Dots failure concerns capture beneath a lip, identity continuity and competing planes. Rowhouse concerns one large persistent apparatus. Safe retries isolates another relationship: repeat an attempted operation without repeating its side effect. It tests whether a clear physical cause and one identity token can carry a new abstract software concept without copying a reference plot.

## Intervention

The new intake explicitly asks for a cause readable in silhouette before mechanisms; one current hero and one rule per setup; stable identity carried by a token, with inactive apparatus removed. The exact script does not prescribe shots. Existing semantic retrieval, independent Reference Director and planner choose the treatment. Canonical puppet/material/font/runtime code may be reused. Production-specific art and adapters remain GENERATED or REVIEW.

## Gates

1. Record exact script/fact packet and live planning receipt.
2. Structure: independent architecture critic; no manual PASS substitutions.
3. Concept: native caption-free representatives and clean BEFORE/CONTACT/AFTER for every interaction in the live contract. Assess acting surface, support, ownership and causal change.
4. Opening: actual 3–5-second rendered native proof in both caption modes, with source hashes, then independent review.
5. Moving rough: actual native full film in both caption modes; independent story/visual critics. Passing structure or concepts does not establish motion quality.
6. Human review: show moving rough and explicit limits. Record rejection as new evidence.

## Readout

Separate plan quality, art implementation effort, native causal clarity, physical contact continuity, story understanding, tactile craft and human entertainment judgment. Record elapsed stages and manual work. No numeric quality average or shot quota. A passed automated gate with a weak movie falsifies its adequacy; it does not redefine success.

## Bounded response

At most two meaningful art/motion repairs within the existing policies. Two repeated concept failures require a different relationship, split or discard. Preserve each attempt. An unavailable tool or incomplete gate is a blocker to promotion, not permission to skip.
