# Motif demonstrated success patterns

Evidence status matters: accepted film, inspected behavior and hypothesis are separate.

- **VoiceStudio v6 — accepted execution benchmark.** Phone-sized moving exports show large tangible interfaces, successive distinct environments, purposeful marquee/headline/caption separation and a consistent canonical Bot. Menu/card/keyboard interactions are supported by the retained film and gold frame ranges. This establishes execution craft when art/choreography are authored, not arbitrary-script autonomy or narrated product accuracy.
- **Little Book Workshop staging — accepted prototype.** Existing approval record and preserved film demonstrate distributing material pieces, parallel work, recombination and a release-before-withdrawal handoff. The same pieces carry identity. Agent-authored geometry/finite adapters are disclosed. The handoff camera hesitation remains a limitation.
- **Calendar/arena regressions — bounded capability.** State validation prevents booking before approval or selection before evidence. Good causal sequencing is reusable; fixed-world templates do not establish reference-level invention.
- **Concept gate/deadlock — demonstrated early stopping.** Both Dots lines preserve blocked evidence and prohibit choreography on unreadable contact. Success is saved production effort and truthful failure reporting, not a better finished movie.

No new R&D concept or generated asset is gold. Append a pattern only after observing it work; record artifact/version, conditions, counterexamples and human approval separately.
