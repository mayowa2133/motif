# Motif benchmark world comparison

[The three-scene comparison](../previews/benchmark-triptych.png) places the approved [night desk](SCENE_01_REVIEW.md), [competition arena](SCENE_02_REVIEW.md), and [code inspection factory](SCENE_03_REVIEW.md) together. All three are approved visual benchmarks. The overall visual system is validated across productivity, selection, and debugging metaphors.

The scenes differ in density and setting: Scene 01 is intimate and plum, Scene 02 is warm and crowded, and Scene 03 is cool and procedural. They share the locked Motif Bot v1, warm cream cut stock, teal success cues, selective coral warnings, shallow front-on staging, small edge drift, fibrous grain and down-right contact shadows. Each software idea becomes a physical event: a stamped finished task, a first-place result, or a bug caught by a scanner.

At 360 × 640, the main result in each scene is readable from large shapes and icons. Small UI and code marks remain environmental evidence. The two new scenes broaden the static visual-system test beyond desk scenes, while timing, transitions and animated material weight still need motion validation. No full prop library is authorized by these scene renders alone.
