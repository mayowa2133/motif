## Recommended first work package: truthful causal waiting

Start with **B1 + B2 + the protocol portion of B3**, followed by one bounded pilot. Do not begin a universal scene schema or broad asset library.

Use a fresh illustrative brief: a visible external request arrives; one worker needs another actor's input; its work stops and stays stopped while input is pending; a visible response permits resumption and produces a retained result. This is a synthetic causal test, not a product claim or a reconstruction of any queued reference.

The first package delivers a mode/evidence manifest, a proposition-to-picture map, a frozen baseline/candidate comparison plan and review forms. Subsequently implement only the smallest scene changes needed by observed baseline failures. B4/B5/B6 provide the narrow execution/readiness work when those failures justify it. Actual capture waits for producer allocation and separate implementation authorization.

Acceptance for the package itself: every script proposition has an explicit evidence route; requested mode cannot be silently substituted; no output claims normal-speed or AV approval from stills. A failed pilot is useful evidence, not a reason to replace the baseline automatically.

## Pilot and transfer protocol to freeze before execution

These are **proposed thresholds**, not validated universal quality norms. Freeze them with the admitted brief before authoring; changes create a new protocol/version.

1. Preserve an unchanged same-brief baseline and candidate inputs, component versions, review policy, artifacts and hashes. Admit only the pending-input hypothesis; no reference exposure. Use the identical requested mode, script, style, narration and delivery geometry in both arms.
2. Inspect every decoded frame in preregistered stimulus/stop/pending/respond/resume and contact intervals. Require zero reproduced critical cause/order, persistent-state, identity, style or mode violations. Unknown/partial coverage is not a pass. Native 360×640 word/diagnostic-detail review is separate from attachment math.
3. Two independent visual reviewers assess the actual artifacts; preserve disagreement. For normal-speed comprehension, recruit **five fresh viewers per arm**, using separate first exposures and no producer rationale. Ask four preregistered questions: what caused the stop, who must act, what persists while input is pending, and what permits resumption. Proposed per-question floor: **at least 4/5 correct**. Captionless-picture, normal-speed-picture and listening/AV results remain separate.
4. AV reviewers must actually watch/listen to the frozen encoded pair and record sync/pacing issues. If unavailable, label AV UNASSESSED and hold whole-film approval. Do not substitute dense still inspection, decode success or alignment measurements.
5. Freeze equal authoring ceilings and a maximum of **two bounded creative repair passes**, consistent with the existing Gold repair policy. Log active minutes separately from elapsed/capture waits, every repair reason, rejected attempt, compute/API usage and priced or unknown cost. Proposed efficiency boundary: treatment median active effort across four scripts ≤1.25× baseline; exceeding it holds an efficiency claim. No speed or cost benefit is inferred from unused ceilings.
6. Transfer success requires all four script pairs to meet scope-specific guardrails, no confirmed comprehension regression, and improvement of at least one preregistered causal question by **≥1 correct viewer out of five in at least two pairs**. Report raw counts by script/question; no invented aesthetic aggregate. Ceiling-level ties support equivalence at most, not a creative gain. A lower count triggers independent recheck before a reproduced regression verdict. Small samples justify a bounded follow-up, not universal reliability.
7. Use PR #8 to compare each eligible addition with original and last good, including combined candidates. A confirmed hard regression disables/reverts that addition in its exact context immediately. Technical validation-only gains may be accepted in their own scope without claiming a comprehension or artistic win. Keep deployment/publication outside this protocol.
