# Star Interaction

Approved Motif visual example; source shot 02, frames [76, 138).

## Why it passes

The landing yields locally; paper stars have a large/medium/small hierarchy. The count and star control remain legible, while supporting motion stays quieter.

## Transferable rule

Use the behavior and focal relationship, not the VoiceStudio wording, product claims or exact layout. Captions support the action. Choose related targets for reactions; keep essential meaning readable at 360 × 640.

## Use

`reference.json` points to the frozen native film and exact frame range. `still.png` is the encoded frame at native resolution. The source video is not duplicated.
